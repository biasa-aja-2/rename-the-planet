# Rename the Planet

[![Rename the Planet](https://make.fun/badge/u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369.svg)](https://make.fun/competitions/u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369)

[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369/biasa-aja-2/rename-the-planet/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/competitions/u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369)

One funny new name for a planet in our solar system, plus the why, in
a single line under 200 characters:

> Mercury is now 'Mood Swing' — minus 180 degrees at night, plus 430 by lunch.

Mercury has the wildest temperature swing of any planet in the solar
system — about −180 °C on its night side and +430 °C in daylight, a
swing of more than 600 degrees — so "Mood Swing" is the name its own
weather earned. The joke is the idea, not a pun on Mercury's name:
the name and the why land together on the first read, and the reason
would not fit any other planet. No rings, no red dust, no
god-of-the-sea gag, no "Uranus" wordplay — just a planet that cannot
decide whether it is freezing or roasting. Pure Python stdlib, no
network, no secrets. `main.py` writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and
verifies it byte-for-byte against a self-check gate (1–200 characters,
single line, names the planet and its new name).

competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).
